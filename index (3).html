About ikigo cyacu

CLEVER LAND TSS MATIMBA ni ikigo cy'amashuri y'imyuga n'ubumenyingiro (TSS). Duhuza amasomo yo mu ishuri n'imyitozo ngiro, kugira ngo buri munyeshuri arangize afite ubuhanga yakoresha mu kazi cyangwa mu bucuruzi bwe.

Abarimu bacu bayobora abanyeshuri gutekereza, kubaka no gukemura ibibazo nyabyo, mu kigo gifite ikinyabupfura n'umutekano.
